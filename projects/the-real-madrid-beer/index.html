THE REAL MADRID
BEER

Madrid has two global icons: its beer and its football club.

Mahou’s ambition in the UK was clear, to be recognised as the authentic beer of Madrid. And there was one advantage no other Spanish beer could claim, its partnership with Real Madrid.

Under the idea The Real Madrid Beer, we brought together two leaders, Spain’s number one beer and the most successful football club in the world. A natural alliance built around a city, a shared origin and an international ambition.

To push the connection further, we introduced Stars belong together a line that turned one of football’s most recognisable symbols into the bridge between both brands. Because when two icons share the same city, the same ambition and the same stars, they belong together.
