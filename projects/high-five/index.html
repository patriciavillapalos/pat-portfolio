How do you show that Goodies is a better alternative without turning its benefits into a list?

We transformed its five Reasons to Believe into a campaign idea that’s easy to recognise and even easier to share: High Five.

The Goodies characters high-five kids to celebrate everything the brand says yes to: real ingredients, less sugar, simpler recipes and nothing unnecessary. A simple gesture that becomes the thread running through the campaign, bringing the product benefits to every touchpoint in a fun and direct way.
